About the group

Substance is found
in the work.

A privately held British group operating and developing specialist businesses across logistics, technology and emerging fields.

A name carried forward.

Silver Lady Holdings takes its name from Stuart Dargue’s first van. What began on the road became the foundation for a wider group of businesses and developing ideas.

The name connects that beginning to the present. The Lady is the group’s symbol: composed, enduring and quietly distinctive.
