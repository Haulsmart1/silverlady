Website privacy.

A concise explanation of this website’s behaviour.

Visiting this website

This website contains information about the group and links to its businesses. The site code does not set cookies, use browser storage or load analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts are provided by your device.

Contacting the group

There is no enquiry-submission form. Email and telephone links open applications on your device. If you choose to contact the group, the information you provide is handled through the relevant communications service.

External websites

Links to group companies take you to separate websites. Their own privacy information applies to those visits.

Questions

For privacy enquiries, contact finance@silver-lady.co.uk.

Hosting logs, email retention and the group’s full personal-data practices must be addressed in the approved privacy notice before publication.
